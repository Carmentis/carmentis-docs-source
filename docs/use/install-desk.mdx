# Get Carmentis Desk

Carmentis Desk is a cross-platform desktop application that handles all common tasks related to
the Carmentis ecosystem. It acts as your **wallet manager** and as a console for application developers and node holders.

![](/img/desk/wallet.png)


## Features

Carmentis Desk allows you to:
- [Manage your wallets](/use/wallets): create or import several wallets, each on its own network, check your balance, transfer tokens and read your transaction history.
- [Manage your organizations, applications and nodes](/use/organizations-and-nodes): create and publish them on-chain, claim a node, stake on it, and anchor certificates.
- [Hold verifiable credentials](/use/credentials): store SD-JWT credentials and present them when an application asks for it.
- [Answer requests from applications](/build/reference/desk-json-rpc): authenticate to a website, sign a message or present a credential, always after your explicit approval.
- Log in to your operators: Desk authenticates you (public-key authentication) to the operator's admin UI, where you configure it.

## First launch

1. **Create your vault password.** On first launch, Desk asks for a password of at least 8 characters. It protects the secrets (such as your wallet keys) stored on your machine.
2. **Unlock Desk.** On the following launches, enter this password to unlock Desk.
3. **Create your first wallet.** See [Manage your wallets](/use/wallets).

## Settings

In **Settings**, you can:
- **Choose your roles.** Desk shows only what you need. Enable *developer* to get the Applications and Custom data tabs, and the Logs. Enable *node holder* to get the Nodes tab. The Organizations tab is shown if either role is enabled.
- **Check for updates.** Desk can download and install a new version, then relaunch.
- **Export or clear the logs** (developer role).
