# Manage proofs

Carmentis provides features to generate and verify a proof of authenticity for a data being anchored on the blockchain.

## Generate proof
The proof generation is performed on your wallet, by clicking on the "Generate proof" button. The proof is automatically
downloaded.
![](/img/wallet-activity.png)



## Verify proof

The verification of a proof can be done at *two* places: on your wallet and on the [explorer](https://explorer.beta.carmentis.io/proofChecker).

![](/img/wallet-verify.png)

![](/img/wallet-verified.png)

:::warning
The verification of the proof reveals information to the proof verifier. Be sure to trust the 
proof checker before to provide the proof file. See more [here](../concepts/proof.md).
:::
