# Declare your oracle

The declaration of an oracle is similar to the declaration of an application. Before to declare an oracle, be sure to
have [created your workspace](./create-workspace).

## Declare a new oracle
To declare an oracle in your workspace follow these steps:

1. Visit your workspace by either clicking on the Workspace link in the [explorer](https://data.testapps.carmentis.io) or directly navigating to https://data.testapps.carmentis.io/workspace.
2. Sign in to your account to gain access to your workspace features.
3. Once signed in, look for the **Oracle** section in the sidebar menu and click on it.
4. To create a new oracle, select the option to add a new oracle. A modal will appear, prompting you to
   enter the name of the oracle you wish to create.
