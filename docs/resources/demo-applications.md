# Demo applications

To experience the Carmentis framework, we have designed an easy-to-understand demo application called **File Sign**.

## File Sign

![filesign](/img/filesign.png)

Connect to File Sign to securely send a file for signature to multiple recipients. Instantly verify signatures on-chain and prove their authenticity with ease.
File Sign makes document signing simple, secure and efficient in just a few steps:
1. **Create an account:** create an account and access your personal dashboard.
2. **Upload documents:** upload the documents you need signed and specify the recipients who need to sign them.
3. **Send for signature:** send your documents to recipients who will receive a notification to review and sign.
4. **Track progress:** monitor the status of your documents in real-time from your dashboard.
5. **Verify on blockchain:** once signed, all document events are anchored on the blockchain for verification.
