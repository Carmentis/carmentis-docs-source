# The applications

In the Carmentis chains, the applications are the services that are offered to the end-users. They are proposed, hosted and provided by the application providers and are accessible through their url.

A service provider is a provider who wants to offer a Carmentis service to its customers (like an [invoicing](https://facturerfacile.fr) service, a contract signing service, etc.).
