# The microchains

The microchain is a private and secure blockchain that is created for a specific instance of a workflow 
(contract signature, quote agreement, invoice payment, etc.)

Each block of a microchain is signed by the service provider and accepted by the end-user on Carmentis side (where he has to log in with an one time password he received by email), and the microchain is stored in the Carmentis Masterchain.
Each microchain is a private and secure blockchain that is created for every specific proof (here a contract signature) and is signed by the service provider and accepted by the end-user on Carmentis side (where he has to log in with a one time password he received by email).
